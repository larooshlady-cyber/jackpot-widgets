Widget & Display

Choose how the jackpot appears to players in your lobby and game pages.

Automatic: all eligible players participate. Manual: players must opt in via the widget.

Floating Widget

Draggable chip overlay that follows the player. Non-intrusive, always accessible. Best for must-drop urgency.

Top Bar

Sticky banner under the header showing live jackpot amount. High visibility, great for lobby pages.

Inline Card

Embedded card in the game grid. Blends with your lobby layout. Best for multi-level displays.

Minimal Counter

Small counter badge in the game header. Subtle, doesn't compete with game content.
